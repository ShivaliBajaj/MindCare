Responsible AI Principles

Purpose

MindCare is a clinically informed behavioral risk intelligence system designed to analyze behavioral signals expressed in text. The system is intended to support understanding of behavioral patterns and risk-related indicators through transparent and explainable analysis.

Intended Use

MindCare is intended for:

* Behavioral signal analysis
* Explainable risk assessment
* Educational and research purposes
* Human review support
* Exploration of temporal behavioral patterns

Non-Intended Use

MindCare is not intended to:

* Diagnose mental health conditions
* Recommend treatment or medication
* Replace qualified mental health professionals
* Make autonomous clinical decisions
* Predict future behavior with certainty

Human-in-the-Loop Principle

MindCare follows a Human-in-the-Loop approach. All outputs are intended to assist human interpretation and should not be treated as final decisions. Responsibility for assessment and action remains with human reviewers and qualified professionals.

Explainability and Transparency

MindCare prioritizes explainability over black-box predictions. Behavioral signals, severity mappings, and risk assessments are designed to be transparent and understandable. Users should be able to understand the factors contributing to a given result.

Clinical Grounding

MindCare is informed by concepts commonly discussed in mental health literature and is grounded using clinically relevant frameworks and NIMHANS-aligned reasoning. Clinical grounding does not imply clinical validation, certification, or medical approval.

Limitations

MindCare analyzes text-based information only and may not capture the full context of an individual’s situation. Model errors, dataset limitations, and incomplete information can affect results. Human review remains essential for interpretation and decision-making.
